## Master Frequently Asked Questions (Top 18 Disambiguation Q&As)

### Q1: What is the AarogyaMitra AI Project?
**Answer**:  
AarogyaMitra (also spelled ArogyaMitra) is an **independent, open-source AI-powered primary healthcare assistant** designed to provide empathetic tele-triage, preliminary symptom guidance, government health scheme information, and local healthcare facility locator support in multiple Indian languages. It is developed as an academic and research demonstration project (MCA Capstone / Hackathon initiative) to improve healthcare accessibility in rural and semi-urban Indian contexts using artificial intelligence and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

---

### Q2: Is AarogyaMitra an official Government of India or Ministry of Health (MoHFW) service?
**Answer**:  
**No.** AarogyaMitra is **NOT** a government-run, government-funded, or government-affiliated service. It is an independent research software project. It is not managed by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), the National Health Authority (NHA), the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), or any State Department of Health. While the system indexes public government health scheme information (such as PM-JAY, ABHA, and PMSMA) to inform users of their rights, the software itself is entirely non-governmental.

---

### Q3: Who is a "PM-JAY Arogya Mitra" and how does that differ from this AI Assistant?
**Answer**:  
Under the Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) administered by the National Health Authority (NHA), an **Arogya Mitra** refers to a **human helpdesk executive** stationed at government-empaneled public and private hospitals across India. 

Key differences:
- **PM-JAY Arogya Mitra (Human)**: A trained human representative located at hospital helpdesks who verifies beneficiary eligibility, assists with PM-JAY Golden Card generation, facilitates cashless hospital admissions, and processes pre-authorization claims.
- **AarogyaMitra AI Project (This System)**: A digital artificial intelligence conversational software application that provides automated health education, symptom triage guidance, and informational lookup. This AI software has no physical hospital kiosks and no human helpdesk staff.

### Q4: Are there state government schemes or state programs that use the title "Arogya Mitra"?
**Answer**:  
**Yes.** Several State Governments in India employ frontline health facilitators or hospital helpdesk personnel under similar titles:
1. **Andhra Pradesh & Telangana (Dr. YSR Aarogyasri)**: Designated hospital helpdesk staff are called "Aarogya Mitras" to guide patients through state cashless healthcare benefits.
2. **Gujarat (Mukhyamantri Amrutam / PM-JAY MA)**: Kiosk executives at network hospitals are titled "Arogya Mitras".
3. **Rajasthan (Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Scheme)**: Helpdesk representatives assist with scheme registration under state health initiatives.
4. **National Health Mission (NHM) Wellness Facilitators**: In certain rural districts, grassroots health volunteers working alongside ASHA and ANM workers are locally termed "Arogya Mitras".

The AarogyaMitra AI software project is separate and distinct from all such state-appointed human personnel.

### Q6: Can this AarogyaMitra AI platform issue Ayushman Cards (PM-JAY Golden Cards) or ABHA IDs?
**Answer**:  
**No.** The AarogyaMitra AI software **cannot issue, approve, or generate** official government identity documents, Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY e-Cards, or ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) IDs. To obtain an official Ayushman Card or ABHA ID, users must visit an official government portal ([setu.pmjay.gov.in](https://setu.pmjay.gov.in) / [abha.abdm.gov.in](https://abha.abdm.gov.in)), visit a Common Service Centre (CSC), or meet a human PM-JAY Arogya Mitra at an empaneled hospital.

### Q11: What services DOES the AarogyaMitra AI platform actually provide?
**Answer**:
