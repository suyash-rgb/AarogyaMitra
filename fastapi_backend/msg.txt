refactor(llm): Implement Multi-Stage Indic Stack Architecture

IndicLID -> IndicTrans2 -> Qwen3.5-2B -> IndicTrans2 multi-stage pipeline

- Integrated IndicLID (Language Identification) script detection across 22 Indic languages and Latin English in app/utils/language.py.
- Refactored app/services/translation_service.py to load AI4Bharat IndicTrans2 dist-200M bidirectional models (Indic -> English & English -> Indic) with transformers compatibility monkeypatches.
- Updated app/schemas/llm.py to support detected_language, translated_prompt, and raw english_response in LLMQueryResponse.
- Refactored app/api/v1/endpoints/llm.py to orchestrate the multi-stage pipeline:
  1. IndicLID detects patient native language.
  2. IndicTrans2 translates non-English prompt to English.
  3. AarogyaMitra Qwen 3.5 2B executes clinical reasoning in English with repeat_penalty=1.15.
  4. IndicTrans2 translates clinical reasoning back to patient's native language.
- Verified end-to-end multi-language queries (Hindi, Bengali) with clean bulleted medical triage responses and automatic evaluation logging.
