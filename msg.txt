Dataset Augmentation for custom dataset

- Implemented LIMA-style strategy (Less Is More for Alignment) favoring strict output formatting over raw dataset size.
- Authored scoring algorithm based on answer word count, sentence count, and punctuation density (commas/colons) to identify step-by-step instructional formats.
- Extracted the Top 50 highest-scoring QA pairs from the refined dataset.
- Unwrapped question arrays (e.g. ["How to treat X?", "What to do for X?"]) to serve as diverse input variants.
- Performed 10x dynamic oversampling by mapping the diverse question variants to the 50 perfectly formatted answers, generating exactly 500 unique QA pairs.
- Applied randomized shuffling to the final dataset to prevent sequential condition bias during epoch training.
- Saved the output as '500_custom_finetuning_dataset.csv'.
- Added 'generate_500_finetuning_dataset.py' script to the data_preprocessing folder for reproducibility.
