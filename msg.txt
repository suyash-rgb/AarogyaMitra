Add ChatDoctor primary care dataset preprocessing script and ChatML dataset export

- Created `preprocess_chatdoctor_dataset.py` in `fastapi_backend/scripts/data_preprocessing/` to filter, clean, and reformat `archive-ChatDoctor` (`HealthCareMagic-100k.json` and `iCliniq.json`).
- Filtered exclusively for 5 common primary care complaints: fatigue, mild fever, back pain, headache, and acid reflux.
- Excluded cases involving oncology, psychiatric crises, or complex inpatient procedures.
- Stripped external doctor signatures, physician names, hospital advertising, phone numbers, website links, and legal disclaimers.
- Restructured doctor responses to consistently ask 1 or 2 targeted diagnostic follow-up questions at the top before offering general self-care advice.
- Truncated verbose clinical essays to concise responses strictly under 130 words.
- Exported exactly 750 ChatML pairs to `datasets/fine-tuning/chatdoctor-clean.json` (150 pairs per complaint category).
- Updated `dataset_preprocessing_report.md` with full documentation and sampling breakdown.
