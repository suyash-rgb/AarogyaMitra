Add FirstAidQA emergency dataset preprocessing script and ChatML dataset export

- Created `preprocess_firstaid_dataset.py` in `fastapi_backend/scripts/data_preprocessing/` to filter, clean, and reformat `firstaidqa_v1.json`.
- Filtered exclusively for high-acuity physical emergencies across 7 categories: cardiac arrest, airway obstruction/choking, arterial bleeding, electrical shock, chemical/thermal burns, fractures, and venomous bites.
- Excluded minor dermatological and non-urgent queries.
- Stripped all conversational fillers ("Hello", "Thank you for your question", "I am sorry to hear that").
- Reformatted all assistant responses strictly into 4 to 5 numbered steps starting with active imperative verbs ("Apply", "Elevate", "Do not", "Position", "Flush", "Immobilize", etc.).
- Appended mandatory standard emergency dispatch trigger: "Call 108/112 immediately if patient is unresponsive."
- Generated and exported exactly 1,000 ChatML pairs to `datasets/fine-tuning/firstaidqa-clean.json`.
- Updated `dataset_preprocessing_report.md` with complete documentation, sampling breakdown, and preprocessing logic.
